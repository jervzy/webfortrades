Pricing questions

Straight answers

Do I pay before seeing the website?

No. We build your demo first and send you a link. You only pay if you want to keep it. No deposit and no risk.

Can I pay monthly?

Yes. You can pay once as a one-off, or spread the cost on a monthly plan from £39 a month. There is no long contract.

Is hosting included?

Yes. Hosting is included with no annual renewal fee on the one-off plans, so your site stays live.

Are changes included?

Yes. Changes and edits are included. Message us and we will update the site, or you can edit the content yourself with the optional editor.

Do I need a domain?

No. We set up a .co.uk domain for you as part of the build. If you already own one, we can use that instead.

Is there a contract?

No long contracts, no retainers and no lock-in. You pay once, or choose a monthly plan you can stop.

Want to see your site before you pay?

Your free demo is ready within 48 hours. No payment upfront, no obligation.
